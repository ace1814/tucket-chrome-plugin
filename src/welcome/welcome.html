Tucket Grab is ready

Grab colours, fonts, SVGs and screenshots from any page. Everything stays on this computer.

  1. Pin it. Click the puzzle-piece icon in the toolbar, then the pin next to Tucket Grab.
  2. Open it on any page, or press ⌥ ⇧ G.
  3. Screenshot straight away: ⌥ ⇧ F full page · ⌥ ⇧ V visible area · ⌥ ⇧ S a selected area. Change them at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.
  • ScreenshotsFull page, visible area or a region. Sticky headers appear once. Mark up and blur for free, then save as PNG, JPEG, WebP or PDF.
  • FontsEvery font on the page, where it comes from, and a link to download it free or find it to buy.
  • ColoursAn eyedropper, and the page’s key colours: the most used, the button colour, and the next one.
  • SVGsEvery SVG on the page outlined. Click one to grab the real vector, not a screenshot of it.

Works with Tucket for Mac

Tucket is a private clipboard manager for designers. Everything you send from Grab lands in it — colours named, icons sorted, screenshots searchable.

Tucket Grab is ready

Grab colours, fonts, SVGs and screenshots from any page. Everything stays on this computer.

  1. Pin it. Click the puzzle-piece icon in the toolbar, then the pin next to Tucket Grab.
  2. Open it on any page, or press ⌥ ⇧ G.
  • ColoursEyedropper, element colours, and the page’s whole palette — including CSS variables.
  • FontsThe family that actually renders, with size, weight and line height.
  • ScreenshotsFull page, visible area or a region. Sticky headers appear once.
  • SVGsThe real vector, cleaned up to work outside the page. Not a screenshot of it.

Works with Tucket for Mac

Tucket is a private clipboard manager for designers. Everything you send from Grab lands in it — colours named, icons sorted, screenshots searchable.

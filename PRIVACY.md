# Tucket Grab privacy policy

Tucket Grab collects nothing.

- It reads a web page only when you open the extension on that page.
- Colours, fonts, SVGs and screenshots stay in your browser until you copy, send or download them.
- Screenshots are kept on your computer, inside the extension's own storage, so the result tab can show them. They are deleted after a day or once five newer captures exist.
- It stores two settings on your computer: your colour format and whether you said you have Tucket.
- It makes no network requests of its own. It has no analytics, no tracking, no accounts and no remote code. When it reads an SVG file, the request goes to the site you're on, the same way the page already loaded it.
- Links to trytucket.com carry a `utm_source` tag, so the website can count visits that came from the extension. Nothing else about you is attached.
